Who we are

A platform for collaborative climate action in Myanmar

UN-Habitat Myanmar advances climate adaptation and nature-based solutions for sustainable and resilient human settlements. In 2023, it established M-CAN for knowledge exchange and collective action, and has since organized Myanmar Climate Action Week every year.

M-CAN is a network of more than 180 non-state climate actors who have voluntarily committed to moving beyond climate discourse to climate action, and to advocating for it.

NGOs, the private sector and community organizations work together to raise awareness, strengthen dialogue and build local resilience, addressing gaps in data, resources and capacity.

Vision

Build a stronger, engaged and inclusive climate network for enhancing multisector climate resilience in Myanmar.

Mission

Advocate, promote and undertake climate actions in Myanmar through partnerships, knowledge generation and dissemination, experience sharing and innovation.
